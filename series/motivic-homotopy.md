# Equivariant motivic homotopy theory

A planned series of papers by Mengzhudong Feng.

## Paper 1

**Motivic Adams isomorphism and tom Dieck splitting in characteristic zero**

Preprint, version 2, released October 18, 2026.

The paper develops a motivic Adams isomorphism, a diagonal twist, isotropy-layer identifications, and a twisted tom Dieck splitting under the hypotheses stated in the manuscript.
